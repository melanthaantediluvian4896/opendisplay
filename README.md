## 🖱️ How to Use opendisplay

After setup, using opendisplay is as intuitive as using a regular monitor:

1. **Change Display Arrangement** – In macOS System Settings > Displays, drag the virtual screen icon to match the physical position of your device (left, right, above, or below your primary monitor).
2. **Extend or Mirror** – By default, opendisplay extends your desktop, giving you more space. If you want to mirror your main screen, just adjust the display settings in macOS and choose "Mirror for opendisplay."
3. **Move Windows** – Simply drag any window past the edge of your Mac's screen, and it will slide over to your iPhone or iPad. Text, images, video—everything transfers seamlessly.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting & Tips

### Can't Find Your Device?
- Ensure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network (or connected via USB).
- Restart both the opendisplay app and your secondary device.
- On your Mac, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen Recording and make sure opendisplay has permission.

### Lag or Low Quality?
- Move closer to your Wi-Fi router or switch to a wired USB connection.
- Lower the display resolution from within the opendisplay app settings.
- Close bandwidth-heavy apps like large downloads or streaming services.

### App Crashes at Launch?
- Update macOS to the latest version (opendisplay supports macOS 12 and newer).
- Re-download the app from the link above, as the file may have been corrupted during download.
